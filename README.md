### About

If you ask me what I love most about programming, the honest answer is: understanding how things actually work under the hood. I'm the kind of person who opens the engine before learning to drive.

I started Computer Science at Unilasalle and quickly realized that sticking to the syllabus wouldn't get me where I want to go. So I do more on the side: certifications, real client projects, AI platforms, English courses, and whatever else shows up. Not out of anxiety — out of genuine interest. Learning something new is what keeps me engaged.

I'm drawn to fullstack because I can't pick a side. Backend has the logic that hooks me, frontend has the visual payoff that's instantly rewarding. I want both.

What I want for the next few years is simple to say and hard to do: become a dev who actually delivers, work with great people, and eventually take this beyond Brazil. I'm not in a rush to be senior tomorrow, but I am in a rush to get better every day.
